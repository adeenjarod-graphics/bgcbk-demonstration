# BGCBK Program Schedule

## Overview

This document presents a sample program schedule for a demonstration model for the Boys & Girls Club of Kings County. The schedule is structured to support youth development, recreation, leadership, and educational engagement.

## Sample Weekly Schedule

### Monday
- 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM: Arrival and check-in
- 9:30 AM – 10:30 AM: Academic support and tutoring
- 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM: STEM exploration
- 11:30 AM – 12:00 PM: Lunch
- 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM: Arts and creative workshops
- 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Recreation and physical activity
- 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM: Mentoring and life skills
- 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM: After-school programming and club activities

### Tuesday
- 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM: Arrival and check-in
- 9:30 AM – 10:30 AM: Reading and literacy support
- 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM: Project-based learning
- 11:30 AM – 12:00 PM: Lunch
- 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM: Leadership development
- 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Creative arts
- 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM: Wellness and fitness
- 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM: Club activities and mentoring

### Wednesday
- 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM: Arrival and check-in
- 9:30 AM – 10:30 AM: Academic support
- 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM: STEM challenge lab
- 11:30 AM – 12:00 PM: Lunch
- 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM: Community engagement discussions
- 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Visual arts and media
- 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM: Team-building exercises
- 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM: Program and recreation block

### Thursday
- 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM: Arrival and check-in

## Programming Priorities

The demonstration program should focus on:

- youth development
- academic support
- leadership and mentoring
- arts and creativity
- recreation and wellness
- community engagement

## Notes

This sample schedule is intended as a proposal for planning and demonstration purposes. Specific sessions may be adjusted to meet the needs of participants and operational capacity.
